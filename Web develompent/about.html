Get to know Us

Sugar Bakes Bakery is a small local bakery that was established in 25 May 2024 by Mr Sugar Mabunda.
Sugar Bakes Bakery has been providing freshly baked good to the community for more than 2 years,
the business offers a wide range of baked goods like bread, rolls, buns etc.
. The business has gained a lot of support from the community and has shown great growth potential and has gained a lot of attraction

Mission

The mission of the business is to sell fresh and affordable baked goods to the not fully developed community

Vision

Is to become a globally known bakery that has worldwide franchisees and one day go back and give to the struggling communities
